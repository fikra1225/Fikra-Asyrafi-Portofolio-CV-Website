Project 01 — Retail Analytics

Maven Market

Power BI Dashboard · 3-Page Report · Multi-Region Retail Data

● Completed

📋 Project Overview

Maven Market is a multi-national grocery chain operating across USA, Canada, and Mexico. This Power BI report delivers a comprehensive three-page business intelligence solution covering retail transactions, product performance, and customer analytics.

Built during the Microsoft Power BI Desktop for Business Intelligence course on Udemy, applying real-world data modelling techniques to a realistic retail dataset.

  • Data Sources: Transactions, Products, Customers, Stores, Calendar tables
  • Data Model: Star schema with dimension and fact tables
  • Measures: DAX calculated columns and KPI measures
  • Visuals: Map, treemap, bar charts, gauge, scatter plot, donut chart
  • Interactivity: Slicers, cross-filtering, drill-through

Data Insights

Key Findings from Maven Market Analysis

🏆

Top Brand Performance

Hermanos leads with 8,071 transactions and $33,167 profit at a 58.54% margin, slightly above the 59.93% fleet average.

🗺

Regional Dominance

USA drives 180.8K transactions. Mexico contributes 72.8K while Canada fills the remaining volume across three countries.

📊

Revenue Seasonality

November peaks at $0.19M monthly while February dips to $0.12M — clear seasonality for targeted promotional planning.

👤

Customer Demographics

91.37% are Standard priority customers. Average age is 81.1 with peak concentration in the 60–80 age band.

🏷

Price Tier Mix

67.6% of products are Low tier; 15.5% Mid and 16.9% High — a value-focused assortment strategy across the range.

♻

Sustainability Score

544 recyclable vs 424 non-recyclable products — Maven Market maintains a 56.2% recyclability rate across SKUs.

Under the Hood

Techniques & Methods Applied

The technical Power BI and data modelling skills demonstrated in this project.

Star Schema Data Model

Connected Transactions fact table to multiple dimension tables with proper one-to-many relationships.

DAX Calculated Columns

Created Profit Margin %, Price Tier classification, and Age Band grouping using DAX expressions.

DAX Measures

Built dynamic KPI measures: Total Revenue, Total Profit, Return Rate, and Revenue vs Target.

Bing Map Visualization

Geo-spatial plotting of store locations and customer distribution across North America and globally.

Conditional Formatting

Color-coded Return Rate column — green for low rates, red for high — enabling instant pattern recognition.

Slicer Interactivity

Region and Member Card slicers enabling dynamic cross-filtering across all visuals on each page.

Calendar / Date Table

Custom date table built with CALENDARAUTO() for accurate time intelligence and monthly aggregation.

Treemap & Scatter Plot

Treemap for regional distribution; scatter plot showing Retail Price vs Product Cost correlation.

KPI Gauge Visualization

Revenue gauge showing $1.76M achieved vs $3.53M target, providing immediate performance context.
