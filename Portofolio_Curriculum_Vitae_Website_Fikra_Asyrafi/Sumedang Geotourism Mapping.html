Geology & Geotourism Assessment in Mount Tampomas, Sumedang, West Java

A geospatial mapping and quantitative feasibility study of five geological sites on Mount Tampomas — combining field geology, petrographic analysis, and a multi-criteria geotourism assessment to recommend an education-based geotrail.

Institution Bandung Institute of Technology (ITB)
Location Mount Tampomas, Sumedang Regency, West Java
Timeframe Oct 2025 – Jan 2026
Role Sole Researcher & Author

The Problem

Overview

Turning a scenic mountain into a data-backed natural laboratory.

Mount Tampomas in Sumedang Regency has striking volcanic landscapes, but until now it has only been used for casual sightseeing — with no formal description, interpretation, or scoring of its geological sites. That gap made it impossible to plan the area's tourism development on solid, measurable ground.

This project inventoried five geological sites around the mountain, reconstructed how each one formed using field description, structural measurements, and thin-section petrography, and then scored every site with a quantitative geotourism assessment method. The result is a set of feasibility scores and a recommended geotrail that can directly inform local tourism planning.

The novelty of the approach lies in combining micropetrographic data with numerical tourism evaluation — a pairing that had never been applied to this area before.

How It Was Done

Methodology

Field and laboratory data feeding into a five-category quantitative scoring model adapted from Kubalíková (2013), weighted and averaged in Excel to produce a final feasibility score per site.

🔎

Scientific & Intrinsic Value

Rarity, geological diversity, site integrity, and how well-documented the site is in scientific literature.

🎓

Educational Value

How clearly the geological process can be understood, and whether the site is actually used for teaching today.

💰

Economic Value

Accessibility, nearby tourism infrastructure, and links to local products and livelihoods.

🌱

Conservation Value

Current site condition, actual and potential threats, and existing legal protection.

✨

Added Value

Cultural significance, ecological role, and aesthetic appeal — color, pattern, and vantage points.

Workflow

Literature & regional geology review → field description and rock sampling at five sites → structural measurements at two waterfalls → thin-section petrography to identify precise rock names → genesis reconstruction → weighted scoring across the five categories → geospatial mapping in QGIS → geotrail recommendation.

A resistant, upright waterfall wall formed when hot pyroclastic material welded into dense crystal tuff. Two joint sets, cutting the rock after it had fully solidified, record later brittle tectonic deformation.

🌴 Ciputrawangi Waterfall

An andesite lava body with striking sheeting joints that mimic layered sedimentary rock — actually formed by pressure release as overlying rock eroded away. Strong scientific value, solid infrastructure, and a local kerupuk opak product make it the standout site.

Geothermal fluid moves sideways through a permeable contact zone between two pyroclastic units rather than rising straight up, leaving a distinctive reddish iron-oxide stain where it reaches the surface. Locals prize it for its perceived healing properties.

The Deliverable

Recommended Geotrail

"An Educational Geotourism Journey Around Mount Tampomas — West, North, to East" — a QGIS-mapped route linking all five sites with photos, descriptions, and scores at each stop.

1. Cikandung Spring

Stop 1

Desa Nyalindung, Kecamatan Cimalaka — 7.5 km from Sumedang city

Swimming, picnicking, and fishing at the source of the Cikandung River.

2. Cipadayungan Waterfall

Stop 2

Desa Padasari, Kecamatan Cimalaka — 3.4 km from Stop 1

Camping in the surrounding pine forest and local Sundanese food nearby.

3. Ciembutan Spring

Stop 3

Desa Cikurubuk, Kecamatan Buahdua — 6.3 km from Stop 2

Swimming in the turquoise spring, passing through open rice fields en route.

4. Cipanas Cileungsing Hot Spring

Stop 4

Desa Cilangkap, Kecamatan Buahdua — 7.3 km from Stop 3

Soaking and relaxing in the hot and cold pools, with full tourist facilities.

5. Ciputrawangi Waterfall

Stop 5

Desa Narimbang, Kecamatan Conggeang — final stop, via the Conggeang kerupuk opak center

Photography and swimming at the highest-scoring site on the route.

So What?

Recommendations

What the scores mean for how each site should be developed next.

🏆

Prioritize the Top Two

Ciputrawangi Waterfall and Cipanas Cileungsing already have the infrastructure and scientific value to lead education-based geotourism development.

📝

Add Interpretation Panels

Cikandung Spring has excellent access but scores low on education — simple geology and hydrogeology signage would close that gap quickly.

⚖

Formalize Legal Protection

Cipadayungan Waterfall has strong scientific and intrinsic value but no legal protection status — a conservation priority despite its lower overall score.

🛣

Improve Access First

Ciembutan Spring's unique turquoise geology is held back by a 1.5 km walk from parking and zero visitor facilities — access should come before promotion.
